(05) How I work

Plain scope, small steps, nothing hidden.

The same four steps whether it's a landing page or a trading system.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A short call or message to pin down what it should do, what it touches and what "done" means. You get a plain-language plan and a clear first milestone.

  2. 02

    Build

    Small working increments you can see early. Config instead of hard-coding, logs from day one, no black boxes.

  3. 03

    Harden

    Dry runs, paper trading or staging before anything touches real money or real users. Edge cases get tested, not hoped about.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Deployed, documented and handed over, with fixes and changes afterwards when you need them.
